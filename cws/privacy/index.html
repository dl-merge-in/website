CWS privacy

This policy applies to the DL Merge-In CWS Chrome extension.

Source-page and media URLs, titles and format information are used for detection, acquisition and source-page navigation. Settings, job information, partial acquisitions and unsaved data are kept in browser storage.

When detecting available streams on Niconico, the extension reads the viewing access key and video ID from the watch page. It sends them, the available video and audio IDs, and a viewing identifier when provided, to the Niconico API at nvapi.nicovideo.jp to obtain an authorized HLS stream URL. Depending on browser settings, the browser may include applicable Niconico cookies in this request.

When player state is available, the extension associates play, pause and end states with detected items in the browser to display playback indicators and order the list. Navigating away or closing the tab clears its detection list from background memory.

Acquisition connects to the content provider or CDN, which receives request information such as your IP address. Opening an external link is subject to that website’s privacy policy.

You can delete unwanted jobs in the manager. Removing the extension may remove its browser-stored data. You manage exported files at their saved location.

Normal media acquisition does not require a developer account or a license check. If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and message and uses them to respond.
